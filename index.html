About Amari Luxe Touch

Where beauty feels like a VIP experience.

Amari Luxe Touch is built for clients who want more than a normal salon visit. We focus on clean beauty, premium finishing, comfort, creativity and service that makes every client feel valued.

  • Luxury comfort: clients are welcomed with tea while they relax and enjoy their appointment.
  • Extra convenience: when a client comes with a dirty car and wants it washed, we can help take it to the car wash while she is at the salon.
  • Beauty services: nails, hair, manicure, pedicure, nail art, make-up application and more.
  • Training: we offer nail tech classes for learners ready to turn passion into perfection.
